  `.btn` and `grid-template-columns: minmax(0, 1fr)` on `.planner-rows`; the Dutch placeholder
  and one long label were shortened so they fit whole rather than being ellipsised.

  because a token override recombines every ink/surface pair and only the combinations that
  actually happen get measured. Dark/nl is clean.

## The limit, stated rather than hidden

The language setting translates the **interface**: the shell, the planner, the picker, the home
screen and its widgets, the departure boards, the stops list, the stop page's headings, the
results screen, the journey cards, the settings screen, every toast, and dates and durations.

Still English: the **demonstration data** — stop names, line numbers, carrier names, and the
fourteen alert bodies — and the longer prose on three screens: `/disruptions`,
`/journey/:id`, and `PreferencesControls` (the mode/walking/transfer controls on
`/preferences` and in the results Options panel). Those are the next pass, and the mechanism
to do it is already in place: one line added to `NL` in `src/lib/i18n.ts` per sentence.

Also worth naming: because the header now sticks *and* paints nothing, content scrolls under
the mark rather than behind a band. That is what a header with no background and a header that
stays put add up to; a row that occludes nothing and stays put cannot be had.
