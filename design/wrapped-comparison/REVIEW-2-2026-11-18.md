# Second read, 18 November 2026

Same method as the read in `REVIEW-2026-11-18.md`, on the film after it was given a beat. Recorded
hands-off at phone size (`artifacts/wrapped-recording/yearbook-portrait-2026-11-18-c.webm`), cut into
**2,000 samples a tenth of a second apart**, and put beside the reference's 1,344 re-measured the same
way. Contract sheets and full-size stills are in `frame-review/ours-c/`.

## What the frames show

The cards move. Every one of them has something travelling for its whole life: the ribbons on the
distance card sweep right across the frame, the tickets on the total card ride up, the slabs on the
direct card climb. Read at a tenth of a second the picture is never the same twice, which is the thing
the first read said was missing, and the deck check now holds every card to it (weakest 0.26, strongest
6.55 out of 255, across a tenth of a second).

The small line works. "7,755 km. That's a lot of window-gazing." with "window seat, obviously" under
it, and the second half swaps in at the halfway point, the written-under-the-picture rhythm the
reference has and ours did not.

**A fault this read found, and fixed.** At full size the small line was ghosting *through* the
headline, both halves half-visible, sitting on the first line, because it was absolutely positioned
against a box whose top edge is inside the headline's own line box. The contact sheets could not show
it; the full-size frame could. It is in the flow under the headline now (`artifacts/wrapped-visuals/
phase5-fix.png`), and the deck check still passes with every ink over 7.3:1.

## What is still different

1. **Still no photographs.** A third of the reference is album covers and a band held for sixteen
   seconds. Ours has no photograph anywhere, and that is why brightness is 124 against 48: it is all
   colour field. This is a content gap, not a code one, it needs artwork chosen by the steerer.
2. **Motion still trails.** 3.29 against 4.84, and a big change every 22.7 s against 7.0. Ours moves
   smoothly and continuously; theirs *cuts*, black dips, palette sweeps, photographs replacing
   colour. Smooth drift is not the same as events.
3. **The music is cut at ten seconds**, because the score was written for fifteen-second cards. The
   visuals are now the reference's pace; the songs are not.

## The score

**7 out of 10**, up from 6 and from the 3 the steerer gave the work before this pass.

Story and chrome 9, pacing 9, movement 6, the written line 8, colour and light 5, photography 2. To
reach eight the film needs photographs on the ranking and one held card; to reach nine it needs those
cuts between scenes, not just drift inside them.
