# OVVO Wrapped share cards: seven cards, five compositions

The share sheet no longer prints one template seven times.

- **Who you were** is a dot-field portrait: one dot for every journey, the name above it, the total below.
- **Your route** is a vertical transit rail with the two stations at its ends and the count riding the line.
- **Your year** is an outlined year over a solid number block, with the three facts on a ledger below.
- **Awards** alternate between a dashed certificate with a round seal and a ticket with a solid stat stub, so four awards do not become four copies.

All seven cards are 1080 x 1920, generated in the browser, and downloadable from the same dialog. The card options are [the contact sheet](contact-sheet.jpg) and the seven full-size PNGs in this folder.
