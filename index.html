This browser is too old for OVVO

OVVO needs iOS 14 or newer on an iPhone or iPad, and on Android or a computer, a browser from 2020 or later. This one cannot read it, so there is nothing to draw on the screen, and no buttons to press, which is why this page looks empty.

A newer phone, tablet or computer will open it normally. Updating the software on this device is the other way, if an update is still offered for it.

Deze browser is te oud voor OVVO. Open OVVO op een nieuwere telefoon, tablet of computer.
